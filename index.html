Netlist Schematic Maker

Paste a SPICE netlist, choose your journal's column width, and download an SVG that is already the right size, with line weights and text set in real points.

Figures and project (several circuits)

A project file keeps every figure with its settings. The PDF has one page per figure, each page exactly the size of its figure.

Where do I get a netlist?
  • LTspice: open your schematic, then choose View, then SPICE Netlist. Copy everything shown and paste it here.
  • KiCad: in the schematic editor choose File, then Export, then Netlist, pick the SPICE format, and open the saved file in any text editor.
  • Other programs: look for an export or "netlist" command and choose SPICE. Menu names change between versions.
  • No simulator: use "Add a part" below. Nothing to type except the part values.
Add a part

Parts: drag onto the grid, or click
Shapes

To wire: click the small circle at the end of a part, then click the end of another part. Drag on empty space to select several parts, or Shift+click. Click a pin and use Ground or Name net to label it. Double-click a part to change its value. Your drawing is saved as a netlist: open the Netlist tab to see or copy it.

These are typical values, not official rules. Check your journal's author guidelines.

Supported: R C L D (LED, zener) V I, Q (NPN/PNP), M (NMOS/PMOS), and op-amps as X1 in+ in- out model or X1 in+ in- v+ v- out model.
